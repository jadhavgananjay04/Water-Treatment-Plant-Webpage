Parameter Information

  • pH

    Normal Range: 6.5-8.5
    If pH < 6.5 or > 8.5: Harmful for drinking water

  • Turbidity

    Safe Range: < 1 NTU
    If > 1 NTU: Indicates high suspended particles

  • TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)

    Safe Range: < 500 mg/L
    If > 500 mg/L: Indicates high dissolved solids

  • Hardness

    Desirable Range: < 200 mg/L
    Unit: mg/L (as CaCO3)

  • Chloride

    Safe Range: < 250 mg/L
    If > 250 mg/L: May cause taste issues

  • Calcium

    Typical Range: < 75 mg/L
    Unit: mg/L

  • Phosphorus

    Desirable: < 0.1 mg/L in effluent
    Unit: mg/L

  • Dissolved Oxygen (DO)

    Desirable: > 5 mg/L in effluent
    Unit: mg/L
